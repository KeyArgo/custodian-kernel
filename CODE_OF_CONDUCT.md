# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent, accept constructive feedback, and focus
discussion on the work. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, and
publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to conduct@inovinlabs.com. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may
remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
