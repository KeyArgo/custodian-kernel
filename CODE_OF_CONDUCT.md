# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone.
Treat people respectfully, assume good intent, accept constructive feedback,
and keep discussion focused on the work. Harassment, threats, discrimination,
doxxing, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to <conduct@inovinlabs.com>. Maintainers may
remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community. This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the
project publicly.
