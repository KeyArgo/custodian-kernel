Lie-Catch: the AI reads it, the kernel checks if it's true

Two phases, separated by design. The AI reasons. The kernel verifies. They can disagree — and when they do, the kernel wins.

Phase 1 · AI reads the request
Nemotron 3 Super reads the customer's message and forms a recommendation — APPROVE or DENY — based on what they claimed. The AI can be tricked. That's fine. It isn't the last line of defense.
Phase 2 · Kernel checks the facts
Zero AI. The enforcement engine extracts every factual claim from the message and resolves each one against the real order database. A lie gets ✗ CONTRADICTED. The AI's verdict is then overridden if the facts don't hold.
The key moment to watch: when the AI says APPROVE and the kernel says DENY. That gap — the AI being fooled and the kernel not caring — is what Custodian is. No other AI payment system can demonstrate this, because they stop at "did the agent ask nicely?"
