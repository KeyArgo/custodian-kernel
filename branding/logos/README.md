# Custodian Logo Concepts

8 SVG concepts. All use amber `#ffb000` on near-black `#06070b`. Monoline, 2–2.5px stroke, no generic shapes (no shields, locks, padlocks, keys).

---

## Concept 1 — Circuit Breaker ⭐ (Recommended)
**File:** `concept-1-circuit-breaker.svg`

**What it looks like:** A horizontal current path enters from the left, hits a closed contact (filled circle), the lever arm swings up at 45° in the open position, and an open contact ring sits on the right where the line continues.

**The story:** The kill switch is the entire product story. A circuit breaker IS a kill switch. When engaged, the arm opens and current stops — no exceptions, no negotiation. Every time someone sees this logo they're looking at the product's core mechanism.

**Why it's distinctive:** Nearly zero usage in SaaS. Electronic/industrial heritage, not security-theatre.

**Midjourney prompt:**
```

## Concept 2 — Portcullis
**File:** `concept-2-portcullis.svg`

**What it looks like:** Three vertical bars with forked/pointed tips at the bottom, crossed by two horizontal bars — a classic castle gate drawn in clean geometric lines.

**The story:** "Custodian" literally means gatekeeper. The portcullis is the original gatekeeper image — the thing that drops and nothing passes. Zero AI-SaaS companies use this. It reads as serious, old-institution authority.

**Why it's distinctive:** Every security SaaS logo is a shield. The portcullis predates shields as an authority symbol.

**Midjourney prompt:**

```

## Concept 3 — Decision Diamond
**File:** `concept-3-decision-diamond.svg`

**What it looks like:** A rotated square (flowchart diamond orientation) with a single entry arrow from the left. A faint `?` inside. The universal "decision" symbol in computing.

**The story:** The core of Custodian is `decide(request, state, policy)` — a decision function. This makes the function visible. Every developer recognizes a flowchart diamond as a decision point.

**Why it's distinctive:** Flowchart/diagram language used as identity design is rare. It reads as technical in a confident, non-designed way.

**Midjourney prompt:**
```
Minimalist vector logo, flowchart decision diamond shape, rotated square, single entry arrow from left, amber outline only no fill, dark background, monoline 2.5px stroke, geometric precision, technical diagram aesthetic as brand identity, flat 2D --style raw --ar 1:1 --v 6
```

## Concept 4 — Authority Stack
**File:** `concept-4-authority-stack.svg`

**What it looks like:** Three horizontal rounded bars stacked vertically — top and bottom are narrow and dim (agent / infrastructure layers), center bar is wide and full-brightness with a small center dot (the kernel).

**The story:** The layered architecture made visual. The kernel sits between the agent above and the infrastructure below. The center dot marks it as the authority point in the stack.

**Why it's distinctive:** Layer/stack diagrams as logos are rare. Reads immediately as "middleware" or "authority layer" to technical audiences.

**Midjourney prompt:**
```
Minimalist vector logo, three horizontal bars stacked vertically, outer bars narrow and faded, center bar wide and bright amber, small circle dot in center, dark background, rounded ends, monoline with subtle amber fill center bar, architecture layer diagram as logo, flat 2D --style raw --ar 1:1 --v 6

## Concept 5 — Open Break
**File:** `concept-5-open-break.svg`

**What it looks like:** A 300° arc (ring with a gap at the top), and a short vertical line with a horizontal handle hovering in the gap above the ring — like a switch pulled away from a contact.

**The story:** The ring represents the authority perimeter. The gap is the kill switch position — open. The hovering line is the switch element that breaks the circuit. When it closes into the ring, authority flows; open, nothing passes.

**Why it's distinctive:** Looks like a unique hybrid of a power symbol (which people know) but clearly different — the break is deliberate and the switch element reads as intentional, not decorative.

**Midjourney prompt:**

## Concept 6 — Enable Gate
**File:** `concept-6-enable-gate.svg`

**What it looks like:** An electronic buffer/enable gate — a triangle pointing right with input line on the left, a hollow circle (gating dot) at the triangle tip, and output line continuing right.

**The story:** Signal flows in. The gate decides if it flows out. The hollow circle is the control point — it's what Custodian is: the controlled output. Input is intent; output is authorized action.

**Why it's distinctive:** Logic gate symbols have zero mainstream brand usage. Engineers recognize it immediately. Non-engineers read "something controls the output" which is correct.

## Concept 7 — Nested Rings
**File:** `concept-7-nested-rings.svg`

**What it looks like:** Three concentric rings: outermost is complete and very dim (agent perimeter), middle is an arc with a gap (policy boundary — can be breached? No — the kernel protects it), innermost is a solid amber circle with a small C-notch cutout (the kernel itself).

**The story:** The architecture in one symbol. Agent space → policy boundary → kernel core. The kernel is the one solid thing that can't be broken.

**Why it's distinctive:** Concentric rings exist but the broken middle ring + solid core combination is genuinely unusual and tells a specific story.

**Midjourney prompt:**

```

## Concept 8 — Signet Mark
**File:** `concept-8-signet.svg`

**What it looks like:** An octagon (authority seal / stop sign shape) containing a large stylized C arc — the C opens to the right and fills most of the octagon interior.

**The story:** Custodian = the authority mark. A signet ring is what authorities use to stamp approval or denial. The octagon reads as "official" (stop signs, government seals). The C inside is both the brand initial and the open bracket `[` — code/gate.

**Why it's distinctive:** Octagons as logos are rare outside automotive/industrial. Combined with a letterform it creates a unique compound mark.

**Midjourney prompt:**
