STEP 1: CREATE A GOOGLE ACCOUNT (1 min)
================================================================
Go to: https://accounts.google.com/signup

Use any Gmail you control, or create a fresh one. Suggested:
  - [PII:email]

You'll need a phone number for verification. Google will text or

================================================================
STEP 2: GO TO YOUTUBE STUDIO (30 sec)
================================================================
Once signed in to your Google account, go to:
  https://studio.youtube.com/

YouTube will ask if you want to create a channel. Click "Create
a channel" or use your existing Google account name.

If you used a new Google account, you'll be the channel owner
automatically.

TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================
- "Handle already taken" → try @Inovin or @InovinLabsAI
- "Phone verification failed" → use a different phone or wait 5 min
- "Upload stuck at 0%" → refresh, try again with smaller file
  (or use a different browser)
- "Video processing error" → re-export from ffmpeg:
    ffmpeg -i custodian-openai-hackathon-final-cinematic.mp4 \
           -c:v libx264 -crf 23 -preset fast \
           -c:a aac -b:a 128k -movflags +faststart \
           reupload.mp4
